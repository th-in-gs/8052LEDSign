PCB milling procedure:
- Avi's bits - V-Bit, 0.1mm 15° (?) for traces.
- Lower the Z until you feel the drag on a piece of paper. Raise it by three 0.25mm increments. Set Z.
- 0.032" (~0.8mm) ball nose for through holes.
- 3m/s
- Find z that drags on paper, shift up 4 times with the minimum movement.
